Water heater repair in Pattukkottai

An electric water heater works with water and electricity together, so faults should be checked properly and safely. We repair storage and instant geysers in Pattukkottai homes. Common problems include:

  • No hot water, or water only lukewarm
  • Takes too long to heat
  • MCB / ELCB tripping when the heater is switched on
  • Water leaking from the tank or connections
  • Indicator lamp not glowing
  • Noise or rumbling from scale build-up
  • Rusty or smelly water

Parts we check and replace

  • Heating element — the most common part to fail, especially with hard water
  • Thermostat and thermal cut-out — control the temperature and protect against overheating
  • Anode rod — protects the tank from corrosion
  • Safety valve and connections — prevent pressure build-up and leaks

We explain what needs to be replaced and the cost before starting.

Water heater sales and installation

Buying a new geyser? We can help you choose a capacity that suits your family and bathroom, and install it with proper mounting, plumbing and electrical connections.

Safety tips

  • Make sure your water heater is properly earthed.
  • If the heater trips the MCB/ELCB or you feel a shock from the tap, stop using it and call a technician.
  • Do not keep the heater switched on for long periods when not needed.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Why is my geyser not giving hot water?

The most common causes are a failed heating element, a faulty thermostat, a tripped thermal cut-out, or a power supply problem. We test each part to find which one has failed.

Why does my water heater trip the MCB or ELCB?

Tripping usually means an electrical leakage, often from a damaged heating element or wiring. Stop using the heater and have it checked — this is a safety issue.

How often should a water heater be serviced?

Where the water is hard, scale builds up on the element and tank. Periodic descaling and a check of the element and anode rod help the heater last longer and heat faster.

Do you install new water heaters?

Yes. We sell and install water heaters, including mounting, plumbing connections and electrical connection.
