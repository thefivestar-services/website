CCTV installation in Pattukkottai

Good installation matters as much as good cameras. A typical CCTV installation from us includes:

  1. Site check — we look at the areas to cover and the best camera positions.
  2. Camera fixing — cameras mounted at the right height and angle to see faces and entrances clearly.
  3. Wiring — cable routing kept neat, using conduit or channels where needed.
  4. Recorder setup — DVR/NVR, hard disk, date and time, and recording settings.
  5. Mobile viewing — live view and playback set up on your phone.
  6. Handover — we show you how to view, play back and save footage.

CCTV camera service and repair

CCTV problems often show up only when you need footage. Call us if you notice any of these:

  • “No video” or a black screen on one or more cameras
  • Blurry, foggy or dark picture, especially at night
  • Recording stopped or hard disk errors
  • Mobile app not showing live view
  • Cameras switching off due to power supply problems
  • Damaged cables after rain, rats or renovation work

We check the cameras, cables, power supply, recorder and hard disk to find the actual cause, explain it to you, and repair or replace only what is needed.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How many CCTV cameras do I need for my house or shop?

It depends on the entrances, the rooms or counter you want to watch and the outside areas such as the gate or parking. A small shop is often covered with a few cameras, while a house with a compound may need more. Tell us about the place, or ask us to come and check, and we will suggest a practical number.

Can I watch my CCTV cameras on my mobile phone?

Yes. Most modern DVR/NVR systems support viewing on a mobile app over the internet. We set up the app on your phone during installation. You will need an internet connection at the recorder, such as broadband or a Wi-Fi router.

How long does CCTV recording last?

Recording time depends on the number of cameras, the camera resolution and the hard disk size. We will explain the options so you can choose a hard disk that keeps footage for the number of days you need.

Do you repair CCTV systems installed by someone else?

Yes. We can check and repair existing CCTV systems — for example cameras showing no video, recording that has stopped, or a mobile app that no longer connects.

Can I add more cameras to my existing CCTV system?

Usually yes, if your recorder has free channels. If it is full, we can explain the options, such as a recorder with more channels.
