Do you provide AC service in Pattukkottai?

Yes. We do AC general service (cleaning), gas top-up / refilling, repairs, installation and shifting for split and window ACs in Pattukkottai. Call or WhatsApp with the AC type (split or window), capacity in ton and the problem, and we will tell you what is needed.

Do you provide CCTV camera installation in Pattukkottai?

Yes. We supply and install CCTV camera systems for homes, shops, offices and other premises in and around Pattukkottai — cameras, DVR/NVR recorder, hard disk, cabling, power supply and mobile viewing setup.

Do you repair refrigerators?

Yes. We service and repair single-door, double-door and other refrigerators — common problems include not cooling, ice build-up, water leakage, unusual noise and the fridge not switching on.

Do you provide washing machine service?

Yes. We service and repair top-load, front-load and semi-automatic washing machines, including problems with water not draining, drum not spinning, noise and vibration, and error codes.

Do you service water heaters?

Yes. We service and repair water heaters / geysers, including heating problems, thermostat or element replacement and leakage, and we also sell water heaters.

Do you sell CCTV cameras?

Yes. We sell CCTV cameras and accessories, and we can suggest the number and type of cameras after understanding the place you want to cover.

Do you provide service for old AC units?

Yes. Older ACs can often be serviced or repaired. After checking the unit we will tell you honestly whether a repair is worthwhile, or whether replacing it makes more sense.

Do you buy or sell used appliances?

Yes. We buy and sell used ACs, refrigerators and washing machines. Availability of used units changes, so please call or WhatsApp to ask what is currently available, or to get a price for your old appliance.
